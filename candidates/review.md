# 480 px review

- **Office:** The launch-day sick note and the boss’s lobby invite form a good two-beat joke. The ghost works the inbox; narrowed the second headline line so it clears the headset.
- **Eviction:** The 214 GB notice and the family photos have a clear cause and effect. The tiny key makes the ghost’s role less immediate than the other scenes.
- **Fraud:** The green confirmation button is the strongest part. The pending charge and the fraud explanation are a looser match than the funeral framing. Made room for the whole headline.
- **Funeral:** The ghost has a reason to be there, and uninstalling a game reads like an absurdly formal cause of death.
- **Round 1:** Specified what the $79.99 bought and made the postponed wake explicit.
- **Round 2:** Moved to a Vice City sunset, brought Jason and Lucia into the estate, and gave the ghost a receipt to stamp.
- **Round 3:** Replaced the long title with an oversized epitaph, clarified the cause of death, enlarged the working ghost, and put the bounty attribution next to the price. The post moves the joke from the game to the family photos.

The third funeral revision was the original editorial selection. It was superseded by `funeral-heirs` in the new run; see the [scoring notes](../README.md#results-and-selection) for why that choice is provisional.

## Launch variants

All previews were checked at 480 × 480, not just at export size. The headstone-sized headline, readable PAID stamp, and separate bill line do most of the work. The crossed-out dates are the smallest and busiest row.

- **Heirs:** The SSD inheritance fits the obituary and the skip-cutscene post adds a different joke. The type fits; the dark footer separates the payout from the condolence copy.
- **Wake:** Keeps the original uninstall joke. The recycle-bin post ties into the eulogy; the compact date row is the least immediate part of the picture.
- **Sick:** “Same lobby” gives the absent pallbearers a reason, but the loading-music post is farther from the wake than the other jokes.
- **Estate:** The strongest completed matchup against #7 in this batch; the long cause-of-death line takes more of the width.
- **Route map:** The navigation arrow and “Turn left in 13 years” read first. The ghost turns the wheel. The map labels take a beat longer than the cost or payout.
- **Custody:** The two-line decree headline carries the joke. The ghost's FILED stamp stays to the right of the settlement rows, where the spacing is tightest.
